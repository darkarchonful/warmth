2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Warmth is a private app for two people in a relationship. Couples fall into routine and struggle to agree on what to do together. Each partner privately swipes through date and activity ideas. Only when both like the same idea does it become a shared Plan, so nobody has to propose something and risk a no. Partners confirm the plan, do it, mark it done, and it becomes a Memory with optional photos and notes. Gentle reminders help them follow through. Audience: adult couples (rated 13+). It is not a social network and never connects strangers: each account links to exactly one partner through a private invite code.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
Custom ideas, comments, photos and notes are visible only to the two partners of one couple. There is no public feed, no search and no way to see or contact any other user.
- Blocking: "Unpair" in Settings immediately ends the connection, removes the partner's access and deletes all shared content.
- Reporting: Settings > "Report a problem or content" and "Contact support" show our support address and open an email or our support page. We respond within 24 hours.
- Account deletion: Settings > Delete account permanently removes the account and all data. Settings is reachable from the deck and from the pairing screen, so accounts that are not paired yet can delete themselves too.
